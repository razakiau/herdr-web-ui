### Changed
- Agent host workspaces open and close as herdr's do: a folder of host chats is a workspace you
  open (**New workspace** → **Agent host** lists the folders, each with its chat count), its tabs
  are the chats you opened, and **Close tab** / **Close workspace** hide without archiving. A chat
  opened from a list is a look until you **Keep open** it or send in it.
- Agent host workspaces share herdr's workspace order: drag them, hold them, or Alt+↑/↓ among
  herdr's workspaces, in Pinned too. The open workspaces, their tabs and the order are kept per PC
  and shared with the server's other browsers.
- The source is named **Agent host** in the "+" dialog (was "Copilot (host)") and the row menu
  says **New agent host chat…** and **Chats in <folder>…**.
